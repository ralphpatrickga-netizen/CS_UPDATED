Four things you can now do from home.

Each one is a form you fill in once. The barangay office in charge receives it directly.

Start with an account

Barangay Clearance

Choose your purpose and see the fee before you submit. Handled by the Barangay Treasurer.

Certificate of Indigency

Pick a purpose, attach what is required, and request the number of copies you need. Handled by the Barangay Secretary.

Certificate of Residency

Proof that you live in the barangay. Your address is filled in from your profile. Handled by the Barangay Secretary.

File a complaint

Describe what happened, add the location, and attach photos or video. The BPSO receives it and you can follow the status.

From sign-up to claiming, in four steps.

No visits to ask where your paper is. The status is always on your dashboard.

  1. 1

    Create your resident account

    Register with your name, address and contact number. Your address is saved so you do not retype it on every form.

  2. 2

    Submit a request with a valid ID

    Fill in the form and upload a photo or PDF of your ID. Someone else claiming for you? Add their details and your authorization letter.

  3. 3

    Follow it from your dashboard

    Every status change comes with a notification and the staff's remarks. Paste a reference number into the assistant to check it instantly.

  4. 4

    Claim it with your slip

    Once approved, open your claim slip. It tells you where to pay and where to pick up your document.

Your request goes straight to the right office.

Each office only sees the work that belongs to it, so your details stay with the people who need them.
