Request details

Certificate of Indigency is issued free of charge.

How to get it: 1) Pay the fee at the Barangay Treasurer Office (free for registered voters) and keep the official receipt. 2) When your request is Approved, claim the certificate at the Barangay Secretary Office and show your receipt. Your uploaded valid ID should show your barangay address.
Free for students, indigents, senior citizens, scholarship applicants, and PWDs — please make sure your uploaded valid ID shows this status.

Uploads & claiming

Government-issued or school ID (if a student). JPG, PNG, or PDF, max 5MB.
e.g. proof of residency, cedula. JPG, PNG, or PDF only, up to 3 files.

Representatives must bring an authorization letter, a photocopy of your valid ID, and their own valid ID when claiming.
